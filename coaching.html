Frequently asked questions

Before You Get Started.

Do I need previous gym or personal-training experience?

No. The programme is designed to support beginners and people returning to exercise. Your starting point will be discussed before onboarding.

Do I need a gym membership?

Not necessarily. Your training approach can be planned around suitable home or gym options, depending on your goals and available equipment.

Will I receive a strict diet plan?

The focus is personalised nutrition guidance and practical food choices that fit your routine, rather than relying on extreme restrictions.

How do I join?

Book a discovery call first. We'll discuss your goals and questions, confirm whether the programme is suitable, and share the next steps if you decide to proceed.
