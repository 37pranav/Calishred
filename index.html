Before you get started

COMMON QUESTIONS.

Do I need gym experience?

No. The programme is suitable for beginners and people returning after a break. Your plan is built around your current ability and available equipment.

Do I need to go to the gym?

No. You can train at home or in a gym. Your workouts are planned around your setup and preferences.

Do I need a strict diet or supplements?

No. The focus is practical, flexible nutrition using accessible food. Supplements are not a requirement.

I'm busy. Can I still follow the programme?

The system is designed to fit real schedules. We build a workable routine around your time, starting point and priorities.

Is this just an online course?

No. It combines a structured plan with coaching calls, progress tracking, personal feedback and ongoing support.

What if I have an injury or health concern?

Share this during your discovery conversation. Training can be adapted where appropriate; medical concerns may require clearance from your healthcare professional before starting.
