Meet Pranav

THE JOURNEY IS THE FOUNDATION.

I'm Pranav, founder of Calishred Fitness. My own training journey began in 2012. Over the years, consistent practice, experimentation and learning have shaped how I think about strength, calisthenics, movement and long-term physical development.

That experience continues to influence my work as a coach. I focus on understanding the person first, building the fundamentals, and creating a plan that can be followed in the context of real life—not just on paper.

Calishred brings that approach together: purposeful training, practical nutrition guidance and a structure that helps people stay consistent, measure progress and keep developing their capabilities.

“You don't need to make fitness complicated. You need to understand the basics, apply them consistently and give the process time.”

Why Calishred exists

PURPOSE.

FITNESS THAT WORKS IN REAL LIFE.

Modern routines often leave little room for movement. People are busy, information is overwhelming, and fitness can become another all-or-nothing project. Calishred was created to make the process clearer: focus on what matters, start from where you are and build capability step by step.

We believe fitness is more than a short-term physical transformation. It is the ability to move confidently, develop strength, improve endurance and maintain habits that support you through changing stages of life.

Calisthenics and bodyweight training are important influences, but the method is not limited to one tool or training style. The right approach depends on the individual, their goals, their experience and the resources available to them.

What guides our work

THE CALISHRED PHILOSOPHY.

Three principles keep the approach focused and grounded.

SIMPLICITY

Train smart, not complicated. Prioritise the fundamentals and avoid adding complexity without a clear purpose.

CONSISTENCY

Progress comes from repeated effort. Build routines that can survive busy weeks, imperfect days and changing schedules.

FUNCTION OVER FLASH

Build a body that performs, adapts and endures. Appearance can be a goal, but capability matters too.
